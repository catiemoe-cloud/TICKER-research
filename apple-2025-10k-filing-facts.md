# Apple Inc. — Company Research Report

**Company:** Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL)  
**Primary source:** [Apple Inc. Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm), filed October 31, 2025 (SEC Accession No. 0000320193-25-000079).  
**Source convention:** Every factual or analytical statement below ends with its source. `UNSOURCED` identifies a statement for which no source was provided or established.

## Business overview

Apple sells technology products, services and software. Its disclosed offerings include smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables and accessories, along with related services; Apple also develops the hardware, operating systems and software applications associated with its products. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1—Business, p. 1; Item 1A—Risk Factors, p. 3.]**

During fiscal 2025, Apple released or announced new offerings in every fiscal quarter: MacBook Pro, Mac mini, iMac and iPad mini in the first quarter; iPhone 16e, iPad Air, iPad, MacBook Air and Mac Studio in the second; new operating systems in the third; and iPhone 17 models, Apple Watch models and AirPods Pro 3 in the fourth. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, “Product, Service and Software Announcements,” p. 21.]**

## Operating performance

Apple’s fiscal-2025 net sales increased in the Americas, Europe, Japan and Rest of Asia Pacific, while Greater China net sales declined. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, “Segment Operating Performance,” p. 22.]**

The Americas recorded $178.4 billion of net sales, up 7%; Europe recorded $111.0 billion, up 10%; Japan recorded $28.7 billion, up 15%; and Rest of Asia Pacific recorded $33.7 billion, up 10%. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, “Segment Operating Performance,” p. 22.]**

Greater China recorded $64.4 billion of net sales, down 4% from fiscal 2024; Apple attributed the decline primarily to lower iPhone net sales, partly offset by higher Mac net sales. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, “Segment Operating Performance,” p. 22.]**

At the product-category level, fiscal-2025 net sales increased for iPhone, Mac, iPad and Services, while Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales decreased. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, “Products and Services Performance,” p. 22.]**

The reported category growth rates were 4% for iPhone, 12% for Mac, 5% for iPad and 14% for Services; Wearables, Home and Accessories declined 4%. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, “Products and Services Performance,” p. 22.]**

## Legal and regulatory exposure

Apple warns that unresolved claims, legal proceedings and government investigations may arise or continue, and that their outcomes are inherently uncertain. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A—Risk Factors, pp. 12–13; Item 3—Legal Proceedings, p. 18.]**

Apple states that unfavorable outcomes could adversely affect its business, results of operations, financial condition and stock price; outcomes above management’s expectations could materially affect the reporting period in which they are resolved. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A—Risk Factors, pp. 12–13; Item 3—Legal Proceedings, p. 18.]**

The filing identifies, among other matters, European Digital Markets Act investigations, a U.S. Department of Justice antitrust lawsuit and litigation involving Epic Games. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 3—Legal Proceedings, pp. 17–18.]**

## Foreign-exchange exposure

Apple faces foreign-exchange risk because it is a net receiver of currencies other than the U.S. dollar. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7A—Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk, p. 27.]**

A strengthening U.S. dollar negatively affects Apple’s net sales and gross margins as expressed in U.S. dollars; the filing also explains that weaker foreign currencies can reduce the U.S.-dollar value of foreign-currency sales and earnings. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7A—Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk, p. 27; Item 1A—Risk Factors, p. 15.]**

Apple uses derivative instruments and may adjust local-currency pricing to manage some foreign-exchange exposure, though it may choose not to hedge certain exposures. **[Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7A—Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk, p. 27.]**
