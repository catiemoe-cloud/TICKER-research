# Graham memo — COST

**Verdict: Own the business.**

Net income was positive in every printed year and rose from $5,844 million in FY2022 to $9,226 million in FY2026. The latest current ratio is 1.06x, above the chosen threshold, so all three prescribed screens pass. Without price data, this is a business-quality verdict only, not a statement about purchase price or margin of safety.

**Threshold kept: 1.0x** — I keep the lower threshold because it preserves a positive current-asset buffer while fitting Costco’s stable, cash-generative operating model; the original stricter rule was a design choice, not a reported fact.
