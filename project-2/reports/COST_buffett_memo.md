# Costco business memo — FY2022 to FY2026

**Judgment: Own the business.**

Costco produced positive owner earnings in every reported fiscal year, rising from $3,501 million in FY2022 to $9,382 million in FY2026. Its ROE remained above the 15% discipline in each year, ranging from 25.11% to 31.19%. Net income also increased from $5,844 million to $9,226 million over the same period. Those numbers support a business that has consistently converted operations into cash and earned strong returns on year-end equity.

This is a business-quality conclusion, not a price judgment. I would watch for evidence that owner earnings or returns on equity weaken; the reported history alone cannot establish how durable the competitive advantage will be.
